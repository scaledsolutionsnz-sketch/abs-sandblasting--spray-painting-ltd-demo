1. Overview

ABS Sandblasting & Spray Painting Ltd respects your privacy. This placeholder policy explains how we handle the information you share when you contact us. It should be reviewed and finalised before the site goes live, in line with the Privacy Act 2020.

2. What we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us when you get in touch — typically your name, phone number or email, and the details of the job you'd like quoted. This website does not use contact forms; enquiries come to us directly by phone or email.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To carry out and follow up on work you engage us for.
  • To keep records for the ordinary running of the business.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We only share it where necessary to complete your job (for example a specialist supplier) or where required by law.

5. Third-party links

This site links to third-party services such as Google Maps and social media search pages. Those services have their own privacy policies, which govern any information they collect.

6. Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it, subject to any records we're required to keep.
