What we do

Surface prep and coatings, done properly.

Bespoke sandblasting, spray painting and galvanising for jobs of every size — carried out at our yard or brought to your site.

01

Abrasive (dry) blasting

Media blasting to strip rust, scale, old paint and coatings back to clean bright metal — the foundation of a finish that lasts.

02

Wet blasting & rust removal

Dust-suppressed wet blasting for delicate substrates and enclosed sites where dry media isn't the right call.

03

Spray painting & protective coatings

Industrial spray finishing and protective, non-slip and specialist coatings applied evenly for real-world durability.

04

Hot arc galvanising & structural steel

Protective galvanising and coating for structural steel, fabrication, flooring and heavy plant and equipment.

05

Automotive & marine refinishing

Blasting and refinishing for cars, trailers, boats and marine gear — the right prep and coating for salt-water life.

06

On-site & mobile blasting

Our mobile blast unit comes to your yard, farm or worksite when gear is too big or awkward to move.

About ABS

A local crew that gets the prep right the first time.

ABS Sandblasting & Spray Painting Ltd works out of Kaikorai Valley Road in Dunedin, handling bespoke sandblasting, spray painting and galvanising throughout Otago and Southland. A flexible approach lets us take on jobs of all sizes — off-site at our yard or on location with the mobile blast unit.

We've built long-term relationships with local engineers, farmers, fabricators and boat owners by keeping things simple: proper surface preparation, honest advice and faster turnarounds.
